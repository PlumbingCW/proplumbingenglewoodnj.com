Before we arrive: burst pipe or active leak in Englewood Cliffs

  1. 1

    Find the right shutoff

    In a house, the main valve is usually where the water line enters, often in a basement or utility closet. In an office, ask building engineering which valve isolates your floor.

  2. 2

    Keep power away from water

    If water is reaching outlets, a panel or floor-level equipment, switch off the breaker only if you can reach it while standing dry. Otherwise stay back and tell dispatch.

  3. 3

    Lift what you can

    Raise files, laptops, rugs and boxes off the floor. Drywall and carpet pad soak up water within hours.

  4. 4

    Clear the way in

    Open a path from the entrance to the meter and the wet room. Tell us about gates, guard desks or parking limits off Hudson Terrace or 9W.

  5. 5

    Call the 24/7 line

    Give the street, the floor and whether water is still running. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.
