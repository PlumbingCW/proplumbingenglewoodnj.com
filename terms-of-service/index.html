Terms of Service

Last updated October 30, 2026.

Using this site

This site gives general plumbing information for homeowners in Englewood, NJ and nearby towns. It is not a substitute for an on-site inspection by a licensed plumber.

Service requests

Requests are made by phone. A plumber will explain the work and give you a clear price before work starts. Appointment times depend on availability, and same-day appointments are offered when available.

Emergencies

If you smell gas, see smoke or fear for anyone's safety, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility first. Do not wait for a plumber.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By calling, you agree to this recording.

Information accuracy

We work to keep our guides accurate, but plumbing conditions, permit rules and prices change. Permit rules vary by job, so check with your local building department before starting work.
