Some jobs live inside the pages above instead of getting their own page. Grease trap cleaning, backflow testing and storm drain cleaning sit on the commercial plumbing page. Cast iron pipe repair sits on sewer line repair, and frozen pipe repair sits on burst pipe repair.

How to choose the right page

Start with what you can see or hear. Water where it should not be, sewage at a drain, or a cold house in winter points to the emergency group. A fixture that works but looks old, a plan to renovate a bathroom, or a wish for more hot water points to the planned group.

If a problem sits between the two, treat it as urgent until a plumber says otherwise. A small drip under a sink can turn into a soaked cabinet floor by morning, and a slow drain can be the first sign of a main line that is about to back up.
