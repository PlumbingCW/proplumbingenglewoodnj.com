The Liberty Pole is one of the oldest named places in Englewood, NJ. The city's history page describes the west end of town as the area around the Liberty Pole Tavern, and the surrounding neighborhood took the same name. Today a Liberty Pole stands near the corner of West Palisade Avenue and Tenafly Road. If you live on the older streets nearby, this page is for you.

The city's Master Plan also lists a Liberty Historic District that includes Cottage Place and Grove, Henry and Charles Streets. It describes smaller houses built in the 19th century by tradespeople, shopkeepers and workers who lived near the village downtown. That is a useful clue about the size of the homes, though it does not tell us what is inside them.

Boundaries here are loose. We use the Liberty Pole corner and those named streets as anchors, and we will not claim to know exactly where the neighborhood ends.

What the history tells you, and what it does not

The first Liberty Pole in Englewood was raised in 1766 to mark the repeal of the Stamp Act. The pole has been replaced several times, and the current one dates from 1964. The nearby tavern gave the area its name, and the corner marks the route the Continental Army used in November 1776.

For a homeowner, the practical point is the age of the settlement, not the stories. Streets laid out this long ago tend to have houses built, extended and renovated in different decades. Two neighbors on the same block may have very different plumbing, so treat any assumption about your own pipes as unconfirmed until someone looks.

If water is running and you cannot stop it

Act in this order. Close the main valve, switch off power only if you can reach the panel while standing dry, and keep away from wet outlets.

  • 1Find the main valve where the water line enters the house, often in the basement or near the meter.
  • 2Move belongings off the floor before drywall and carpet soak up water.
  • 3Photograph the damage for your insurer before you clean up.
  • 4Call and tell us the street, the room and whether the valve closed.
