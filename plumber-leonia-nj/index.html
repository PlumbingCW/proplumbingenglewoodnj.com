Winter: when pipes freeze and split

Freeze damage hits pipes in exterior walls, unheated garages, crawl spaces and outdoor hose bibs first. When overnight temperatures stay below freezing, open the cabinet doors under kitchen and bath sinks on outside walls, and let a faucet drip. Disconnect garden hoses in autumn and close the interior valve that feeds each hose bib.

If a pipe does burst, close the main right away and open a faucet to relieve pressure. Never use an open flame to thaw a line. Call us for burst pipe repair, and keep the water off until the plumber confirms the damaged section is fixed.

Before we arrive: burst pipe or active leak in Leonia

  1. 1

    Shut it off at the main

    Find the main shutoff before you need it. In a cold snap, closing it quickly can keep a split pipe from flooding a finished room.

  2. 2

    Turn off the water heater

    If the heater is in the flooded area, switch off its electric breaker or set a gas unit's control to pilot only, per the label. Do not touch a wet appliance.

  3. 3

    Do not thaw with flame

    Skip torches and heat guns near a frozen pipe. They start fires and can make a split worse.

  4. 4

    Check the low points

    On Leonia's west side, water settles fast in basements. Move boxes, rugs and stored items up before you wait.

  5. 5

    Call and describe it

    Tell dispatch your street, which wall the pipe is in and whether the water has stopped. If you smell gas, leave first and call 911 or the utility.

Old houses ask for careful plumbing

Leonia was incorporated in December 1894, and its oldest dwelling, the Cole-Allaire House, dates to about 1765. The Vreeland House followed in 1786 and was expanded in 1815. Most homes are far newer, but the borough is old enough that you should expect a mix of materials inside walls.

Plaster walls, narrow crawl spaces and mixed pipe types slow a repair and raise the cost of opening walls. Ask for a clear price before work starts, and ask where access holes will be cut and how they will be patched.

Street trees and the sewer lateral

Tree City USA streets look good, but roots find small leaks at lateral joints and grow inside the pipe. Signs include a toilet that gurgles, a drain that clears and returns in a few weeks, and a wet patch over the line in the yard. A hydro jetting service can clear root growth and grease, though a camera should confirm a collapse or offset first.

Routine care helps. Keep grease out of the kitchen drain and flush only toilet paper. Near Wood Park, Sylvan Park or Overpeck County Park, expect more roots and more moisture in the soil.
