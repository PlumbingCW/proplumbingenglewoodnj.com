The ground matters as much as the age. East Hill rises sharply east of downtown toward the Palisades, and water behaves differently at the top of a street than at the bottom.

Water on a slope: East Hill versus downtown

Water pressure at a house can differ with where it sits on the slope. A house low on the hill may see higher pressure that wears out faucet cartridges and flexible hoses early, while a house near the top may notice a weak shower. Before replacing fixtures, ask a plumber to read the pressure at an outdoor hose bib. A gauge reading takes two minutes and rules a lot out.

Surface water also runs downhill. After heavy rain, basements near the base of the slope can take on seepage, which makes a working sump pump matter more than it does on flat ground. Pour a bucket of water into the pit this fall and watch the pump start. If it does not, sump pump repair and installation is a small job compared with a flooded finished basement.

What a long-settled town means for your pipes

Tenafly was incorporated in 1894, so it is reasonable to expect some older houses here, though we cannot tell the age of yours from the address. Houses that were never updated can still have galvanized steel supply lines, cast iron drain stacks or clay sewer laterals. Orange-brown water, pressure that drops at a single fixture, or a gurgling drain are the usual hints.

A camera look through the main drain, as described under sewer line inspection, shows the pipe condition without digging. Ask for it before you agree to a large repair.

Before we arrive: burst pipe or active leak in Tenafly

  1. 1

    Close the main

    Turn the main shutoff to closed. A wheel handle turns clockwise, and a lever handle ends up crosswise to the pipe. It is usually near the water meter, often in the basement or a utility closet.

  2. 2

    Check the lower floors

    Water finds the low point. Look in the basement and any walkout level for pooling before you trust that the leak is where you first saw it.

  3. 3

    Protect the panel

    Do not walk through standing water to reach a breaker box. If the panel is wet or the floor is flooded, stay out and call us and the utility.

  4. 4

    Drain the line

    Once the main is closed, open the lowest cold faucet in the house. It drains the water still sitting in the pipes and slows the leak.

  5. 5

    Phone the 24/7 line

    Tell us your street and whether the house is uphill or downhill of downtown. A gas smell means leave and call 911 or the gas utility first.

Wooded streets near the Nature Center and Lost Brook

Tenafly Nature Center sits at 313 Hudson Avenue and adjoins Lost Brook Preserve, which was bought in 1975 under the Green Acres program, on East Hill. Streets near that much woodland have mature trees, and mature trees send roots toward any joint that weeps moisture. A sewer lateral that keeps backing up every year or two is a likely candidate, so ask about sewer line repair before paying for repeated cleaning.

The same trees drop leaves and needles into yard drains and gutters. Clear them before storms so roof water does not spill toward the foundation.
