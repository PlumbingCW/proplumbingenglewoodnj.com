Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Englewood Pro Plumbing, 5 E Palisade Ave, Englewood, NJ 07631. You can reach us at contact@proplumbingenglewoodnj.com or by phone at (551) 249-3045.

How we take requests

We take plumbing requests by phone only. The site has no forms, so it does not collect typed information from visitors. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information you give us by phone

When you call, we ask for your name, the service address, a callback number and a description of the problem. We use that information to dispatch a plumber, give you a price and follow up on the job.

Website data

Like most sites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We use them to keep the site secure and working. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you or to delete information we hold about you by emailing contact@proplumbingenglewoodnj.com. Tell us the phone number or address you used when you called.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
