Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Englewood, NJ

Water pouring from a broken pipe? Licensed plumbers stop the flow, repair copper, cast iron and frozen pipes, and protect your home across Englewood.

A burst pipe can release hundreds of gallons into a house within an hour, so speed counts and our line is open 24/7. Burst pipe repair in Englewood, NJ means stopping the flow, finding the failed section of supply or drain pipe, and replacing it so the line holds pressure again. The steps below take about a minute and can limit damage before a plumber arrives.

Pipes fail in different ways. Copper pinholes from corrosion, a split from a hard freeze, a cracked cast iron stack, a broken supply valve and a loose fitting each need a different repair, and the cause tells us whether more of the pipe is at risk.

Signs a pipe has failed

Some bursts are loud and some are slow, so look for these.

  • ✓Water spraying, dripping through a ceiling or pooling under a sink
  • ✓A sudden drop in water pressure across the house
  • ✓Stains, bubbling paint or warm spots on a wall or floor
  • ✓The sound of running water when everything is off
  • ✓A water meter that keeps turning with all fixtures closed
  • ✓Rusty or discoloured water, or a damp smell in a closet

Stop the water now, then protect the house

Go straight to the main water shutoff. It is usually where the supply enters the house, often in the basement near the meter. A wheel handle closes clockwise, and a lever closes with a quarter turn. Learn its location before you need it, and if it will not turn, call us at once.

Once the water is off, open the lowest faucet in the house and a high one to drain what remains in the pipes. If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, do not touch them. Shut off power only at a dry panel, and otherwise call the electric utility or 911.

  • 1Close the main shutoff valve, then open taps to drain the lines
  • 2Switch off the water heater power or set its gas control to pilot per the maker's instructions, so it does not run dry
  • 3Stay out of rooms with water near live outlets or a wet panel
  • 4Move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the wet area
  • 5Photograph and video the damage and the pipe before cleanup, for your insurance claim
  • 6Mop up standing water only where it is clearly away from live power
  • 7Keep the broken section if you can, since it helps show the cause

Copper pipe repair

Copper is common in homes of many ages, and it fails in a few predictable ways. Tiny pinhole leaks usually come from corrosion inside the pipe, while a long split along the side points to freezing. A single pinhole may be fixed with a short replacement, though several holes mean the line is wearing out.

Repair means cutting out the bad length and joining new pipe with soldered fittings or press fittings. We confirm the pipe is dry and the system depressurised first. If a whole run is wearing out, a repiping project may serve you better than patching every few months.

Cast iron and drain pipe breaks

Cast iron drain stacks and house drains carry waste, so a break may leak sewage and not clean water. Older cast iron rusts from the inside, develops scale and cracks near joints. Signs include slow drains, sewer odour, stains around the pipe and wet spots in walls or under the slab.

A short cracked section can be cut out and replaced with plastic pipe and heavy rubber couplings, but pipe that is scaled throughout may fail again elsewhere. A camera inspection shows how much of the line is sound. Buried drain breaks overlap with sewer line repair, which we handle as well.

Frozen pipes and what happens when they thaw

Pipes most likely to freeze run along exterior walls, through unheated crawl spaces and garages, and into outside faucets. Water expands as ice forms and can split the pipe, but the leak often only shows once the ice melts.

If you find a frozen pipe, open the nearest faucet, then warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer starting at the faucet end. Never use an open flame, torch or heater that can start a fire. If the pipe is behind a wall or you cannot reach it, call us, and keep the main shutoff in reach in case it splits.

Access

Extent of the damage

One short section costs less than a long run or several failed sections.

Water cleanup

Time of day

Night and weekend callouts may cost more than a scheduled daytime visit.
